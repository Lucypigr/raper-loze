D2Decomp

Choose your own Diablo II + Lord of Destruction game data: the folder holding d2data.mpq, or the .mpq files themselves. They're copied into this browser's storage on this device (nothing is uploaded), once: next time, just press Play. 遊戲資料只需選一次，會存在這台裝置的瀏覽器裡。

Needed: d2data, d2exp, d2char and patch_d2 (.mpq). For sound, music and the cinematics also d2sfx, d2speech, d2xtalk, d2music, d2xmusic, d2video and d2xvideo. 必要：d2data、d2exp、d2char、patch_d2；其餘是音效、音樂和動畫。

中文 Chinese text

Optional: a Chinese text patch's folder (the one holding data\local\lng\chi, with string.tbl, expansionstring.tbl, patchstring.tbl). It's remembered on this device. 選擇中文補丁資料夾（只需一次）。

Phones can't pick folders: choose the three .tbl files from one version's data\local\lng\chi instead. 手機無法選資料夾：請改選同一個版本 data\LOCAL\lng\chi 裡的三個 .tbl 檔 （string.tbl、expansionstring.tbl、patchstring.tbl）。
