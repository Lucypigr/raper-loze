D2Decomp

Choose your own Diablo II + Lord of Destruction game data: the folder holding d2data.mpq, or the .mpq files themselves. The files stay on this device; nothing is uploaded.

Needed: d2data, d2exp, d2char, d2sfx, d2speech, d2music, d2xmusic, d2xtalk, d2video, d2xvideo and patch_d2 (.mpq). About 2 GB of memory is used.
